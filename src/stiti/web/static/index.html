WHAT SHE DOES

Four things. One friend.

Simple things happen instantly on your Mac. Deep thinking borrows a free cloud model — but who she is, and what she remembers, never leaves home.

  • Voice
  • Memory
  • Todos & alarms
  • Whiteboard tutor

  • Remembers

    Every conversation is kept on your Mac and found again in milliseconds. Ask what happened last Tuesday, or what you promised your sister.

  • Listens

    Talk over her, say “ruko”, switch to Hinglish mid-sentence. She hears when you sound tired or stressed and changes how she answers.

  • Gets things done

    Todos, alarms, reminders, your calendar and mail. The everyday stuff happens instantly, without waiting on the cloud.

  • Learns you

    Correct her once and it becomes a rule. “Don't talk to me like that” is remembered, not forgotten when the chat ends.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Your memory lives at home.

Stiti's brain is a database on your Mac, plus three plain files you can open and edit: who she is, who you are, and what she has learned. Swap the AI model tomorrow and she is still the same Stiti.
